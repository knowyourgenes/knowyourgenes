Are your genes lowering your testosterone?

You're 28. Same gym, same food, same sleep, but you're tired in a way rest doesn't fix, and you've stopped mentioning it.

Yes. Some men are born with genes that lower hormone production, hitting energy, muscle, mood and sex drive as early as their 20s. Know early, and you stop blaming stress, age or "a bad phase" for something you can actually fix.

Hair Fall · AR, LINC01432, C1orf127

Is your hair loss coming from your genes?

More hair on the pillow than usual. You tell yourself it's stress, a new shampoo, the weather. It's probably none of those.

Yes. One gene in particular, called AR, is one of the strongest signs of future hair loss in men. Most treatments work best before hair loss shows. Know early and you keep every option open.

Thinning at crown Patchy spots Hair pulls out easily Receding line
warningOnce a hair root dies, no treatment brings it back. Timing is everything.

Can your genes make it harder to have a baby?

You've been trying for a baby for eight months. Every month feels heavier than the last, and neither of you knows why.

Yes. A man can look perfectly healthy and still have genes that make it harder to have a baby. Knowing before you try means fewer months lost, less blame between partners, and a specialist sooner if needed.
