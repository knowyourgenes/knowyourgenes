add_photo_alternateHyperuricemia visual
Uric acid crystallising science
Hyperuricemia · Gene ABCG2

Is my body clearing uric acid properly?

Uric acid that isn't cleared efficiently crystallizes, showing up as gout or as stones inside the kidney. ABCG2 governs how well your body clears it.

warningWatch for: gout, kidney stones.
Sample reading
Poor · High
add_photo_alternatePolycystic visual
Fluid-filled cysts over time bubble_chart
Polycystic Kidney Disease · Gene PKHD1

Could cysts be forming in my kidneys?

An inherited tendency toward fluid-filled cysts forming in the kidneys and slowly crowding out working tissue. PKHD1 flags your inherited risk.

warningWatch for: side pain, blood in urine, repeat UTIs.
Sample reading
Good · Low
add_photo_alternateMagnesium visual
Minerals held, or lost electric_bolt
Hypomagnesemia · MUC1, ATP2B1, DCDC5, SHROOM3, CNNM4

Are my kidneys holding on to magnesium?

Magnesium keeps muscles, nerves and heart rhythm steady, and your kidneys decide how much of it you keep. Five genes shape how well yours hold on.

warningWatch for: cramps, numbness, abnormal heart rhythm.
Sample reading
Good · Low
add_photo_alternateChronic Kidney Disease visual
Filtering capacity, slipping monitor_heart
Chronic Kidney Disease · NMT2, APOL1

Am I losing filtering capacity quietly?

The gradual loss of filtering capacity, often silent until it is already advanced and much harder to slow. NMT2 and APOL1 shape your inherited risk.

warningWatch for: fatigue, nausea, swollen feet, high blood pressure.
Sample reading
Average · Medium
add_photo_alternateNephrotic Syndrome visual
Protein leaking through child_care
Childhood Nephrotic Syndrome · TNFSF15, HLA-DQA1

Could protein be leaking into my urine?

A tendency toward protein leaking out through damaged filtering units, most commonly seen in children. TNFSF15 and HLA-DQA1 carry the signal.

warningWatch for: protein in urine, puffy eyes, swollen ankles.
Sample reading
Average · Medium
add_photo_alternateMembranous Nephropathy visual
Filtering vessels thickening shield_with_heart
Membranous Nephropathy · HLA-DQA1, PLA2R1

Could my immune system thicken the filter?

An immune-related thickening of the kidney's tiny filtering vessels, which slowly lets protein escape. HLA-DQA1 and PLA2R1 are the clearest markers.

warningWatch for: swollen legs, foamy urine, fatigue.
Sample reading
Good · Low
add_photo_alternateKidney Stones visual
Minerals hardening into stone emergency
Renal Calculi · CLDN14, CASR

Am I prone to forming kidney stones?

How readily your kidneys turn dissolved minerals into hard deposits, and how much pain that eventually costs. CLDN14 and CASR set the tendency.

warningWatch for: blood in urine, severe pain, nausea.
Sample reading
Good · Low
