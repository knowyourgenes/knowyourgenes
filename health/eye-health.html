Diabetic Retinopathy · VEGFA, ICAM1, EPO

Is my retina at risk from diabetes?

Long-term high blood sugar slowly damages the blood vessels of the retina, often with no symptoms until real vision loss has begun. Three genes show how your retinal vessels hold up.

warningNo symptoms until real vision loss has begun.

Glaucoma · MYOC, LOXL1-AS1

Could pressure be harming my optic nerve?

Rising pressure inside the eye damages the optic nerve so gradually that peripheral vision is often gone before it is ever noticed. MYOC and LOXL1-AS1 flag your inherited risk.

warningPeripheral vision can be gone before it is noticed.
Sample reading
20/20 · Good

Cataract · Gene EPHA2

Will the lens in my eye slowly cloud over?

A slow clouding of the eye's natural lens, building over months and years rather than announcing itself all at once. EPHA2 is linked to inherited forms of cataract.

warningIt builds silently over months and years.
Sample reading
20/70 · Average

Myopia · Gene TGFB1

Why does my distance vision keep blurring?

Nearsightedness: sharp up close, blurry at a distance. Common, well understood, and among the easiest conditions here to correct early. TGFB1 shapes how your eye grows and focuses light.

warningLeft unchecked, it can keep worsening for years.
Sample reading
20/20 · Good

Pressure with no symptoms monitor_heart
Ocular Hypertension · Gene PTGFR

Is my eye pressure quietly too high?

Higher-than-normal eye pressure with essentially no symptoms of its own. The single biggest known precursor to glaucoma. PTGFR affects how your eye pressure is regulated.

warningThe single biggest known precursor to glaucoma.
Sample reading
20/200 · Poor
add_photo_alternateMacular Degeneration visual
Central vision breaking down filter_center_focus
Macular Degeneration · ARMS2, C2, VEGF

Could I lose my central vision as I age?

A breakdown of central vision, the part used for reading and recognizing faces. One of the leading causes of vision loss after 50. ARMS2, C2 and VEGF sit behind the retina.

warningA leading cause of vision loss after 50.
Sample reading
20/25 · Good
add_photo_alternateRetinal Occlusion visual
A stroke at the level of the eye emergency
Retinal Occlusion · AGTR1, ADIPOQ

Am I at risk of a sudden retinal blockage?

A sudden blockage of blood flow to the retina, effectively a stroke at the level of the eye. It calls for immediate attention when it happens. AGTR1 and ADIPOQ affect vessel and metabolic health.

warningIt is a medical emergency when it happens.
Sample reading
20/70 · Average
