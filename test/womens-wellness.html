PCOS · Gene THADA

Do my genes put me at risk of PCOS?

PCOS happens when your hormones go out of balance. It affects your periods, your weight and your skin. The THADA gene tells you how likely you are to get it.

Left alone, it can lead to diabetes.

Could I have trouble having a baby?

Some women cannot conceive even after years of trying, and no one can tell them why. Two genes affect folate and how your immune system reacts in pregnancy.

Mood · Gene COMT

Could pregnancy affect my mental health?

Some women feel very low and anxious while pregnant, or after the baby comes. The COMT gene shows how well your body handles stress hormones.

Half of it begins before the baby is born.

Bones · AKAP11, LRP5, ZBTB40

Will my bones get weak as I get older?

After the age of 30, your bones slowly begin to lose strength. There is no pain, and no warning sign. Three genes show how fast yours may weaken.

Most women find out when a bone breaks.

Joints · Gene HLA-DRB1

Could my immune system damage my joints?

In rheumatoid arthritis, your immune system attacks your joints and slowly damages them. The HLA-DRB1 gene is the clearest warning sign.

Joint damage cannot be undone.

Who should take this test

This test is for every woman. But it might be for you most of all.

Your genes never change, so testing once protects you for life. Some women, though, have every reason to know sooner rather than later.

This test is for you if…

  • Your periods have never been regular, and no one has ever really explained why.
  • You are thinking about a baby — now, or somewhere down the line.
  • You have lost a pregnancy, and were never given a real reason why.
  • PCOS, thyroid, weak bones or arthritis runs in your family.
  • You often feel low, anxious, or not quite yourself — around your cycle, or after a baby.
  • Your joints ache or stiffen more than they should for your age.
  • You are in your 20s or 30s and feel perfectly fine — which is exactly when acting early works best.
  • You are simply tired of guessing, and want clear answers about your own body.

If even one of these is you, knowing now gives you years to act instead of regret.
