Test 02 / Hair Fall
Hair Fall · AR, LINC01432, C1orf127

Is your hair loss coming from your genes?

More hair on the pillow than usual. You tell yourself it's stress, a new shampoo, the weather. It's probably none of those.

  • Yes. One gene in particular (called AR) is one of the strongest signs of future hair loss in men.
  • Most treatments work best before hair loss shows. Know early and you keep every option open.
