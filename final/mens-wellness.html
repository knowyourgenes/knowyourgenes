Test 01 · Hormones · LOC108783645, HFE

Are your genes lowering your testosterone?

  • Yes, they can. Some men are born with genes that lower hormone production as early as their 20s.
  • It hits energy, muscle, mood and drive.
  • Know early, and you stop blaming stress or age for something you can fix.

Test 02 · Hair Fall · AR, LINC01432

Is your hair loss coming from your genes?

  • Very likely. The AR gene is one of the strongest signs of future hair loss in men.
  • Most treatments work best before hair loss shows.
  • Know early and you keep every option open.
Thinning crownPatchy spotsReceding line
Once a hair root dies, no treatment brings it back. Timing is everything.
Test 03 · Fertility · Gene ART3

Can your genes make it harder to have a baby?

  • Yes. A man can look perfectly healthy and still carry genes that make it harder.
  • Knowing before you try means fewer months lost.
  • Less blame between partners, and a specialist sooner if needed.
