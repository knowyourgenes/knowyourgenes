What we check

Five risks that stay silent until it is too late.

Here is what each one checks, with a real sample result.

Test 01 · PCOS · Gene THADA

Do my genes put me at risk of PCOS?

  • PCOS happens when your hormones go out of balance.
  • It affects your periods, your weight and your skin.
  • The THADA gene tells you how likely you are to get it.
Left alone, PCOS can lead to diabetes.
Test 02 · Pregnancy Loss · MTHFR, FOXP3

Could I have trouble having a baby?

  • Some women cannot conceive after years of trying, with no answer.
  • Two genes affect folate and the immune response in pregnancy.
Most women never get a real reason why.
Test 03 · Mood · Gene COMT

Could pregnancy affect my mental health?

  • Some women feel very low or anxious while pregnant, or after.
  • The COMT gene shows how well your body handles stress hormones.
Half of it begins before the baby is even born.
Test 04 · Bones · AKAP11, LRP5, ZBTB40

Will my bones get weak as I get older?

  • After 30, bones slowly lose strength, with no pain or warning.
  • Three genes show how fast yours may weaken.
Most women find out only when a bone breaks.
Test 05 · Joints · Gene HLA-DRB1

Could my immune system damage my joints?

  • In rheumatoid arthritis, your immune system attacks your joints.
  • The HLA-DRB1 gene is the clearest warning sign.
Joint damage cannot be undone once it starts.
